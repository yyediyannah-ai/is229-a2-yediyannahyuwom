Welcome to BrightPath

BrightPath Learning Centre helps primary and secondary students build confidence in maths, science and English through small-group tutoring and personalised study plans. We started in a single rented classroom in 2019 with six students, and have since grown into a trusted after-school option for families across the local community, without ever losing the small-group, personal approach we started with.

Why Families Choose Us

  • Qualified, subject-specialist tutors
  • Small class sizes of no more than six students
  • Flexible after-school and weekend sessions
  • Regular progress reports for parents

Quick Facts

Open Monday to Saturday, 2pm–6pm. Sessions run in 1-hour blocks. New students can book a free trial lesson via our enrolment page.
