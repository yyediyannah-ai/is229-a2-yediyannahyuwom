Our Mission

We believe every student can succeed with the right support. BrightPath was founded in 2019 to give local families access to affordable, high-quality tutoring outside regular school hours.

Our Approach

Every student starts with a short assessment so we can build a study plan around their actual needs, not a one-size-fits-all curriculum. We also check in with parents every four weeks with a short written update, so families always know exactly how their child is progressing, not just at report card time.
